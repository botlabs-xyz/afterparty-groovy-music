## FAQ

### Does it support direct song links?

Yes, direct URLs are supported through `/play` where source support is available.

### Is this for playlists or single tracks?

Both are supported depending on source and deployment settings.

### Why does playback sometimes stop?

Typical causes include source availability, voice disconnects, or permission changes.

### Where do I get support?

Use the official [Support Server](https://discord.gg/BusuZp2G8w).
